# Public show study and original Helix quality targets

Access date: 2026-10-18. Scope: publicly accessible creator descriptions and
indexed video metadata. Direct YouTube playback was blocked by a human
verification page in this browser. **No frame-by-frame viewing, global popularity
ranking, auditory analysis, or timestamped choreography comparison was completed.**
No video, captions, paid sequence, proprietary timeline, or creator-specific
choreography was downloaded or stored. These are generalized engineering targets,
not a recipe for reproducing a show and not measured vendor performance values.

## Accessible public evidence

| Example | Evidence actually retrieved | Implication for evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tom BetGeorge, “2024 Christmas Light Show — Full Show!” | Public YouTube index reports 2,439,235 views; description credits multiple programmers and separate hardware, editing, laser and firework roles. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iDyt0xgYQNM | Evaluate the composition and reliable execution as separate systems; scale or equipment count alone does not prove sequencing quality |
| Living Light Shows / Matt Johnson | Creator site describes synchronized musical compositions, an EDM background, public interactivity and more than 3.5 million views in 20 days for one residential show. https://livinglightshows.com/about-us/ | Require coherent musical structure across the whole display and optional audience interaction; do not treat brightness/density as the sole success metric |
| Trans-Siberian Orchestra, “Wizards in Winter” | Public search identifies the official music-video lineage and the familiar synchronized residential display; direct player unavailable. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pWBjl-jPcVM | Include a simple-channel baseline; a complex layout must demonstrate stronger musical communication rather than merely more pixels |

Counts are source/index snapshots, not an independently established most-viewed
list. These examples do not establish that Helix has matched or surpassed them.
Direct viewing and independent scoring remain open in the master roadmap.

## Proposed, testable Helix targets

| Dimension | Proposed gate | Current evidence / gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rhythmic precision | At 50ms frame resolution, source attack shifts never exceed one frame; evaluate detector precision/recall separately against labeled recordings | Semantic compiler does not shift original musical cues; audio detection accuracy still requires labels |
| Instrument independence | Keep drum, guitar, bass, keyboard and mallet lanes separate; exact parity outside explicit semantic scenery | Existing lane regression tests plus native parity gate; full-band comparisons still pending |
| Singing timing | Human-labeled word median boundary error <=80ms, 95th percentile <=150ms; reported phone errors only for measured alignments | Contract fixtures only; dictionary phone boundaries explicitly estimated; WER/boundary accuracy unmeasured |
| Silence and uncertainty | No invented text, phonemes, independent harmony or semantic story during unsupported spans | Confidence/unknown-span fixtures; real recognizer errors still need independent annotation |
| Visual hierarchy | One semantic focal idea per phrase, with musical accents retaining separate lanes; assess focal readability blind at viewing distance | Bounded semantic intent/target scope; blind visual scoring pending |
| Emotional range | Sections differ in palette, motion direction, scenic spread and restraint when evidence supports a change | Continuous dimensions and transformed-motif tests; real-song review pending |
| Narrative consistency | Repeated imagery has stable identity, contextual transformations and an explicitly justified resolution | Negation, metaphor-abstention, mixed-mood and motif fixtures; complex narratives unresolved |
| Spatial adaptation | Same musical clock across simple AC and rich RGB layouts; use only supported props/effects | Scenic RGB compiler verified structurally; AC semantic composition and richer effects remain open |
| Export integrity | XSQ imports/rendering; full decode and original-audio correlation >=.98; no lost instrument channels; native LMS validation separately | Workflow checks native FSEQ/MP4; LMS regression tests do not establish LOR application import |
| Artistic quality | Blind paired listening/viewing by at least three reviewers; publish scores and disagreements across multiple genres | Not yet performed; no “better than vendors” claim |

To exceed these examples, Helix must demonstrate those properties consistently
across unfamiliar owner-authorized songs and layouts, including sparse music,
humor, conflicting mood, long quiet passages and phrase repetition. Increasing
effect variety without measured musical fidelity does not satisfy that objective.
