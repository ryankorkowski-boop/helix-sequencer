# HELIX BETA RELEASE v1.0

Welcome to Helix. Audio in. Lights out.

Helix represents numerous hours of independent development by Ryan Korkowski over approximately a year. Its mission is to make sophisticated music-responsive lighting accessible to homeowners and hobbyists, with free noncommercial use. These are plans, not delivery-date promises. Thank you for testing Helix and reporting problems with your app version, selected mode, error message and reproduction steps. This is beta software; use a preview and check imports and channel mappings before operating a show.
