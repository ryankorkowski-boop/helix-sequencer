# Showcase 12

Twelve synthetic residential displays, drawn as a street-facing elevation in inches. They are generated by `tools/showcase12/generate_layouts.py`. No private layouts or songs are included. `house.json` is the unlit house silhouette composited behind real FSEQ pixels.

| Panel | House |
|---|---|
| s01_ac16 | 40-foot one-story ranch, center gable, one channel per roof edge, window, and shrub |
| s02_ac64 | Two-story colonial with a dormer, upper windows, porch, fence, and candy-cane walk |
| s03_ac256 | Wide three-gable estate, every window and stake on its own AC channel |
| s04_rgb_porch | The same ranch in pixels at about 3-inch spacing |
| s05_arches_faces | Ranch plus yard arches, spinners, a candy-cane walk, and a porch singing face |
| s06_pixel_yard | More arches and spinners, two porch faces, a singing tree, and a porch matrix |
| s07_mega_yard | Ranch with a 16-foot, 16-string mega tree and star in the front yard |
| s08_busy_rgb | Taller facade, four arches, four spinners, porch faces, and two singing trees |
| s09_mega_grove | Two-gable house, 16-foot and 20-foot mega trees with stars, spiral evergreen |
| s10_helix_tunnel | House plus a driveway of arches and two helix-wrapped trees |
| s11_matrix_wall | Two-story house with a pixel-matrix garage door and window matrices |
| s12_band_stage | Ranch on the left, side-yard stage with snowmen, backdrop matrix, drums, and an 18-foot mega tree |

Singing faces use an xLights NodeRange `faceInfo`. Run with `--sync-lyrics-heads`.
