# Mesmerizing geometry concepts · 63–82

Twenty original light-layout concepts exploring linked curves, minimal-surface-inspired forms, aperiodic patterns, recursive volumes and carefully phased illumination. Each paired plate presents two different hero scenes. The catalog records geometry, artistic intent, motion ideas and the important construction question for each design.

These are visual concept studies. Motion descriptions are sequencing proposals; this batch does not create native xLights models, sequences or MP4s. Familiar mathematical forms guide the artwork rather than establishing exact mathematical or fabrication geometry.

Open `index.html` for the mobile review gallery or the PNGs in `images/` for full-size paired plates. IDs continue after the existing 1–62 catalog. All earlier artwork, approved shows, audio and finished drummer assets remain unchanged.
