63 · Hopf Halo

Linked circular fibers

A luminous sphere resolves into hundreds of linked circles, with slow color currents slipping between them.

Light motion: Phase-offset chases travel around hoop families, then a dim-to-bright meridian reveals the linked layers.

Geometry and construction

A monumental spherical sculpture of many discrete linked circular LED hoops, each tilted on its own axis, a nested orbital center and a clear dark void through the middle. Jade, pearl and restrained amber; a museum courtyard with tiny people and grounded black supports. The overall mass is a sphere but individual linked circles remain readable.

A Hopf-inspired finite circle arrangement; intersecting hoops, maintenance access and supports need actual design.
