How to read these concepts

The spatial round separates foreground, middle distance, background and overhead layers. Later sequencing could use depth waves, vertical sweeps and diagonal or radial paths. The abstract round explores moiré, parallax, finite repeating structures and phase-shifted patterns on static frames.

These images illustrate artistic intent. Model spacing, sightlines, pixel density, physical supports and native model mapping still need design work if a concept is selected. No moving machinery or holograms are required by the briefs.
