# Twenty-two Helix concept layouts

**Concept images only — awaiting user selection.** These proposals are deliberately not xLights layouts, native model exports or rendered song sequences. Eleven paired high-detail image plates contain twenty-two individually numbered concepts. The offline gallery shows each concept separately without altering the original image files.

Open `index.html` to browse the seven rounds, enlarge a concept and keep a local shortlist. Shortlist choices stay in your browser; use **Copy shortlist** to share the numbers and names. Browsing or shortlisting does not build anything.

These effects come from static models, layered light and viewing angle. Animation can suggest growth, rotation, expansion and interference without moving machinery. Their appearance depends on future spacing, sightlines, brightness and sequencing. The images illustrate the intended look; they are not measurements or proof that an optical effect will survive a particular pixel density or camera angle.

## Files and preservation

- `images/`: eleven unmodified generated plates and three copied existing favorite reference images.
- `concepts.json`: twenty-two briefs, planned spatial choreography, panel IDs and image metadata; no model coordinates or channel plan.
- `generation_prompts.json`: the eleven original generation prompts.
- `index.html`: self-contained offline review catalog with no external services.
- `verification.json`: image decode/link/archive and favorite preservation checks.
- Existing Aurora / Fire & Ice / Enchanted Grove selections and baseline hashes: `../../favorites/selected_layouts.json` in the repository.
- Local downloads: `outputs/layout_concepts/Helix_22_Concepts_Review.zip` and `outputs/layout_concepts/Helix_Saved_Favorites.zip`.

All three favorite native folders remain byte-identical to commit `15a7e5450d2798eacc57b821267d6a526428884a`. Other existing layouts, the five-song batch and all drummer behavior are preserved. No new XML, XSQ, FSEQ, XMODEL, OBJ, native render or audio sequence is produced by this concept round. No CI/native workflow is dispatched for artwork.

Physical dimensions, site conditions, supports, terrain/scenery, power, controllers, wiring and pixel counts are intentionally unresolved. Canyon terraces and other large scenic structures could instead be reinterpreted as simpler supported tiers during a selected design's refinement. Engineering and native model-family coverage belong to that later build phase.

Build only the concepts the user selects. Do not infer approval from plausible art or a local shortlist. Track future implementation and acceptance in `MASTER_TODO.md`.
