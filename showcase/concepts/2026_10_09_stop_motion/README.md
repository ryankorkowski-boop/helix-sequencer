# Helix stop-motion concepts — 43–62

Twenty new concept scenes on ten paired plates, with a four-frame illustrated motion study for each. All are artwork awaiting selection. No native xmodels, layout XML, XSQs, FSEQs or MP4s are created for this round.

## Core idea

Use successive stationary light poses, replacement contours and staged illumination to suggest movement in discrete steps. Anticipation, contact, recovery, held poses, squash/stretch, overlapping action and loops supply the motion language. Handcrafted miniature staging supplies the look. The storyboard cells are illustrative keyframes, not a timed animation or proof that the pictured hardware moves.

## Approval boundary

Reply with IDs to approve, reject or revise. Opening an image is not approval. Prior advanced-perspective concepts23–42 retain their existing pending status.

After selection, determine whether each design uses separate pose-frame models, overlapping submodels or an actual display surface. Choose contact/rest frames, pose spacing, held exposure cadence, inactive-frame darkness and viewing angle before native model construction. Native rendering and motion readability would then require separate checks; the art does not establish those.

All proposals assume static light structures sequenced between poses, not automatic motors or deforming physical props. The carousel is zoetrope-inspired pose choreography rather than a tested spinning zoetrope. Drummer55 illustrates motion/stage ideas, not replacement canonical drummer geometry or a source-song performance. Existing musical/drummer acceptance remains separate.

Original art, native layouts, source media, favorite baselines and canonical drummer assets remain unchanged. See MASTER_TODO.md for continuity.
